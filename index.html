About QuestBlox

QuestBlox is a growing browser-game hub designed to make discovering and enjoying games simple. Explore different genres, jump into creative challenges and find new favourites without installing a separate app.

We're building more than a collection of game links. Our plans include player profiles, verified quests, XP rankings, a virtual Bucks economy, friend connections, private friend messaging and multiplayer features in games that support them. These features will be released progressively and tested before they go live.

Creators will also be able to submit their own games for manual review, helping QuestBlox grow while keeping publication in the developers' hands.

QuestBlox is developed by Viaan, Aadvik and Arsh.
